  • Hong Kong’s free harbour-and-city highlights
  • Nauryz festivities in Almaty
  • Ala-Archa’s mountain scenery

What does not fit

  • Mainland China: visa + two extra days

02 · Day-by-day

Hong Kong to Almaty

DAYS 01—06

Two efficient city days, one airport night, then four nights in Kazakhstan during its spring celebration.

D01THU · 18 MAR
Jakarta → Hong Kong

Fly to HKG. Take the airport bus to Sham Shui Po; explore street markets and eat at a local cooked-food centre.

Sleep · Hong Kong hostel
D02FRI · 19 MAR
Harbour on foot

Star Ferry, Central, Tsim Sha Tsui and Victoria Harbour. Walk up Victoria Peak rather than paying for the tram. Overnight at HKG.

Free sleep · Airport landside
D03SAT · 20 MAR
Fly into Central Asia

Published CX681: Hong Kong 16:25 → Almaty 20:30. Reconfirm the schedule before booking. Airport bus or Yandex to the hostel.

Sleep · Almaty
D04SUN · 21 MAR
Nauryz in Almaty

Join public Nauryz festivities, then visit Panfilov Park, Zenkov Cathedral and Green Bazaar. A high-value, mostly free day.

Sleep · Almaty
D05MON · 22 MAR
Medeu without the splurge

Take the city bus toward Medeu and enjoy the foothills. Skip the Shymbulak cable car unless the reserve remains intact.

Sleep · Almaty
D06TUE · 23 MAR
Slow city, cheap views

Arbat, bazaars and Kok-Tobe on foot. Charyn Canyon is optional and deliberately excluded from the base budget.

Sleep · Almaty
Airport-night reality. HKG is open 24 hours, but landside seats are noisy and often have armrests. Bring earplugs and an eye mask. A second hostel night adds about Rp450K.

02 · Day-by-day

Mountains to the Silk Road

DAYS 07—12

Two land borders, a mountain day and a night bus reposition you from Kazakhstan to Uzbekistan.

D07WED · 24 MAR
Almaty → Bishkek

Morning bus from Sayran through Qorday. Take every bag through immigration. Evening at Ala-Too Square and Osh Bazaar.

Sleep · Bishkek
D08THU · 25 MAR
Bishkek, local style

Explore Osh Bazaar, central squares and neighbourhood canteens. Confirm your Tashkent bus and terminal location today.

Sleep · Bishkek
D09FRI · 26 MAR
Ala-Archa day trip

Use the city bus and park shuttle. March snow means choosing the river or waterfall trail only if conditions are safe.

Sleep · Bishkek
D10SAT · 27 MAR
Night bus to Tashkent

Slow morning and food preparation. Depart around 19:00 from the temporary terminal; cross Kyrgyzstan–Kazakhstan–Uzbekistan borders overnight.

Free sleep · Overnight bus
D11SUN · 28 MAR
Tashkent essentials

Chorsu Bazaar, Hazrati Imam and the city’s ornate metro stations. Use the metro rather than airport or station taxi touts.

Sleep · Tashkent
D12MON · 29 MAR
Ordinary train to Samarkand

Take Sharq or another cheaper service. Settle into the old town, then enjoy the Registan exterior and evening atmosphere.

Sleep · Samarkand
Border habitPassport, e-Visa and QazETA stay on your person—never in the bus luggage hold. Carry small KZT and UZS notes for roadside stops.
Mountain habitCheck the shuttle and weather before leaving. Early spring trails can be snowy, cold and avalanche-prone above the easy routes.

02 · Day-by-day

Samarkand, Bukhara, home

DAYS 13—18

Concentrate paid entries on the monuments that matter most. Use a sleeper train for the return to Tashkent.

D13TUE · 30 MAR
Samarkand’s greatest hits

Registan and Gur-e-Amir, Bibi-Khanym exterior and Siyob Bazaar. Add Shah-i-Zinda only if the daily allowance remains.

Sleep · Samarkand
D14WED · 31 MAR
Train to Bukhara

Travel on a cheaper ordinary service. Spend the afternoon around Lyabi-Hauz, the old lanes and covered trading domes.

Sleep · Bukhara
D15THU · 01 APR
Old Bukhara

Visit the Ark fortress, see the Kalon complex, Chor Minor and old-town sunset. Much of the city’s appeal is free to walk.

Sleep · Bukhara
D16FRI · 02 APR
Sleeper back to Tashkent

Slow morning, then choose an evening night train around 21:35–23:15. Book the sleeper as soon as the sales window opens.

Free bed · Night train
D17SAT · 03 APR
Tashkent → Jakarta

Fly home, preferably on a single TAS–KUL–CGK booking. Keep the fare below Rp5.75M and avoid unprotected tight self-transfers.

Free sleep · Plane / airport
D18SUN · 04 APR
Arrive Jakarta

End of the 18-day route. Keep any untouched reserve as the trip’s final saving—not a reason for a last-day shopping spree.

Hong Kong & Almaty

Cooked-food centres + stolovayas

Use supermarket breakfasts and Hong Kong cooked-food centres. In Almaty, point-and-eat stolovaya meals commonly run 1,000–2,500 KZT; samsa and bakery food keep breakfast cheap.

Bishkek & Uzbekistan

Canteens + one-dish meals

Choose plov, manty, lagman and samsa where locals eat. Published Uzbek main-dish prices are around 51,000–53,000 UZS. Skip cafés, alcohol and tourist-facing set menus.

Bed check

Read reviews from the last six months. Hostels in Central Asia can close while old blog recommendations remain online.

Tax check

Uzbek tourist tax is often collected separately at check-in. The budget includes approximately Rp350K across five nights.

Sleep kit

Earplugs, eye mask, padlock, travel towel and a warm layer for buses. Keep passport and valuables attached to your body.

Two travellers? Compare a basic double room before booking two dorm beds—especially in Uzbekistan. Solo travellers usually save more with dorms; pairs sometimes do better with a guesthouse.

06 · Entry & admin

The paperwork is part of the route.

INDONESIAN PASSPORT
Visa-free · 30 days

Hong Kong

No advance visa for an Indonesian ordinary passport. Carry the onward HKG–ALA ticket and accommodation details.

Official Immigration dataset ↗
Visa-free · 30 daysQazETA required

Kazakhstan

Apply for free QazETA at least 72 hours before entry. Valid for 180 days. Although mandatory enforcement begins in July 2027, the rollout reaches Almaty Airport in January and all borders in March—obtain it before this trip.

Official QazETA procedure ↗
e-Visa required

Kyrgyzstan

Budget approximately USD70 / Rp1.28M for single-entry tourism. Apply only through the official government portal. Review can take five working days after all corrections; mistakes and refusals are non-refundable.

Official Kyrgyz e-Visa portal ↗
Visa-free · 30 days

Uzbekistan

Your accommodation must register your stay. Keep registration records and expect tourist tax to appear separately on the bill. Both registration and tax are different obligations.

Official MFA visa list ↗
Brunei · optional

Visa-free · 14 days

Only relevant if added on a Royal Brunei through-ticket. Check the final entry rules before departure.

China · alternative only

Regular tourist visa required

The 240-hour transit scheme excludes Xinjiang. Do not attempt Yining on transit-free entry.

Official advice worth following: Kyrgyzstan’s portal advises against buying flights before visa approval. Secure the e-Visa before committing to non-refundable long-haul tickets.

Yining–Almaty bus details ↗
Optional · +Rp0.8–1.2M

Brunei stopover

Royal Brunei accepts eligible 8–24-hour stopovers on selected city pairs. Request at least five working days ahead; RB Super Saver is excluded. The published terms do not promise free accommodation and put stopover-related costs on the passenger.

Cheap visit: Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque exterior, Kampong Ayer and Tamu Kianggeh. Indonesian passports receive 14-day visa-free entry.

Official stopover terms ↗
Beijing

Wrong direction, wrong trip length

Another major long-distance leg plus at least two useful sightseeing days. It does not improve the Central Asia overland logic.

Dushanbe

A detour with backtracking

Adds border crossings and at least two days around the Samarkand corridor. Save Tajikistan for a longer Pamir-focused trip.

Best fallback: remove Hong Kong and Kyrgyzstan, keep Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, and use Kuala Lumpur for both international connections. Expected total: approximately Rp19–21.5M.

08 · Booking battle plan

In the right order.

NEXT ACTIONS
  1. Confirm the passport and fare ceilings.
    This plan assumes an Indonesian ordinary passport and Rp12.5M total airfare.
  2. Start the Kyrgyz e-Visa.
    Use only the official portal; check every passport character before paying.
  3. Book the three international legs.
    Open-jaw route. Prefer a protected TAS–KUL–CGK itinerary.
  4. Reserve refundable dorms.
    Check recent reviews, location and tourist tax before paying.
  5. Set the Uzbek train reminders.
    Start checking around late January; buy ordinary trains and the Bukhara sleeper when inventory opens.
  6. Submit QazETA.
    Free, online, at least 72 hours before entering Kazakhstan. Do it earlier.
Non-negotiable

Leave with Rp1.5M+ untouched.

If final bookings cannot preserve a meaningful reserve, switch to the fallback route instead of hoping nothing goes wrong.

Carry-on strategy

7–10 kg · laundry once or twice · no “just in case” items. Checked luggage can consume most of the reserve.

March / April pack

Thermal layer, compact insulated jacket, waterproof shell, walking shoes and warm socks. Mountains remain wintry while Uzbek cities turn mild.

Money & apps

2GIS · Yandex Go · offline translation · some USD cash · international debit/credit card. Decline dynamic currency conversion; pay in local currency.

The verdict

This budget works—with discipline.

The 18-day route is feasible when all three flights stay below Rp12.5M, the bag stays in the cabin, and optional tours stay optional. You still get Hong Kong, Nauryz, the Tien Shan and the Silk Road’s two great cities.
